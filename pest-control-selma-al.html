Local conditions

What we see on Selma properties

Older housing stock with crawlspaces, pier foundations and original wood framing gives subterranean termites more ways in and more to eat, and makes moisture control a bigger part of the job.

Long, humid summers and mild winters mean a longer active season here than most of the country, for termites, mosquitoes and roaches alike.

Properties backing onto wooded lots or the river bottom see more rodent and wildlife pressure, especially as the weather turns.

Real estate closings in Dallas County commonly need a wood-destroying insect report on a deadline, which is worth starting early rather than the week of closing.
