About Veterans Pest Control

Service built on experience, integrity, and local accountability.

We are a locally owned, veteran-led pest control company based in Selma, Alabama, serving residential, commercial, and industrial customers.

Our story

A local team with a responsibility to serve well.

Veterans Pest Control LLC was founded by Eddie Bennett, a U.S. veteran and certified pest control operator with more than 17 years of industry experience.

Our work is grounded in the same values that guide good service anywhere: show up, communicate clearly, do the work carefully, and stand behind the relationship.

From annual inspections to urgent pest concerns, we help customers across Alabama and neighboring parts of Georgia protect the places where they live and work.

What matters to us

Professional without losing the personal touch.

  • Respect for your home, business, family, and customers
  • Recommendations based on the actual property and issue
  • Plain-language communication before and after service
  • Long-term relationships over quick transactions
