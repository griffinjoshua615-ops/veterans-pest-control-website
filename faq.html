How do I know if I have termites?

The usual signs are mud tubes running up foundation walls or piers, wood that sounds hollow when tapped, floors or trim that feel soft, discarded wings near windows and doors, and winged swarmers indoors in spring. Termites work out of sight, so by the time damage is visible the colony has usually been active a while. If you see any of these, get it inspected rather than waiting.

How do I know if I have bed bugs?

Check mattress seams, box springs, and headboards for small dark spots, shed skins, and live insects. Bites alone are not a reliable sign, since people react very differently and other insects bite too. Bed bugs travel in luggage and secondhand furniture, so finding them says nothing about how clean a home is.

What happens on the first visit?

We inspect first. That means finding where the activity is, how pests are getting in, and what conditions are supporting them, such as moisture, clutter, or gaps around the structure. Then we explain what we found and what we recommend, and you decide. We do not treat before we have looked.

Is the treatment safe around children and pets?

Before we treat, we tell you which products we are using, where they are going, and any instructions for staying out of an area or keeping pets away until it dries. If anyone in the home is pregnant, has asthma or other respiratory conditions, or you keep fish, birds, or reptiles, say so up front so we can plan around it.

How often do I need service?

It depends on the pest and the property. General pest pressure is commonly handled on a recurring schedule through the year, because a single treatment does not stop new pests from moving in from outside. Termites and bed bugs are handled differently and are not on the same cycle. We will tell you what your situation actually needs rather than defaulting everyone to the same plan.

What should I do before you arrive?

Clear access to the areas of concern, including under sinks, along baseboards, and around the exterior foundation. Put away food, pet bowls, and children's toys in the rooms being treated. Write down where and when you have seen activity, since that detail helps us find the source faster.

Do you work with businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle commercial and industrial properties including restaurants, warehouses, offices, and schools, where the requirements around documentation and scheduling are usually different from a house. Tell us what your operation needs and we will build service around it.

I am buying or selling a house. Do I need a termite inspection?

Lenders and buyers commonly require a wood-destroying insect inspection report as part of a real estate closing in Alabama, and there is usually a deadline attached. Tell us your closing date when you call so we can work to it. Confirm the exact report your lender or agent requires, since requirements vary by loan type.
