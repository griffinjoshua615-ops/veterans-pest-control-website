Ants

Ant Control in Selma & Central Alabama

Ants are the most common call we get, and the species decides the answer. Fire ants are a yard and safety problem. Carpenter ants are a wood problem. The small ones trailing across the counter are a food and moisture problem. Same word, three different jobs.

Signs to look for

How to tell if you have ants

  • Persistent trails along counters, baseboards or the foundation line
  • Loose fire ant mounds in the yard, with no visible entry hole, that erupt when disturbed
  • Coarse sawdust-like shavings, sometimes with insect parts, near trim or window frames, which points to carpenter ants
  • Large black ants indoors at night, especially in spring
  • Ants concentrating around sinks, dishwashers or bathrooms, which usually means water rather than food

Why it matters here

What ants mean for an Alabama property

Fire ants are a real hazard, not a nuisance

Imported fire ants are established across Alabama. The mounds turn up in lawns, pastures, playgrounds and around utility boxes, and they sting aggressively when disturbed. Treating one mound at a time tends to move the colony rather than remove it.

Carpenter ants mean moisture

They do not eat wood, they hollow it out to nest in, and they overwhelmingly choose wood that is already damp. Finding carpenter ants indoors is often the first real sign of a leak nobody had noticed.

Trailing ants are following a resource

A trail is a supply line to food or water. Killing the ants on the trail does nothing to the colony, which is why the trail reappears in a day or two somewhere slightly different.

Before we get there

What you can do yourself

None of this replaces treatment, but it reduces the pressure and makes the work hold longer.

  • Wipe trails with soapy water to break the scent line, which helps temporarily but does not treat the colony
  • Store sugar, pet food and open packets in sealed containers
  • Trim shrubs and tree limbs so they do not touch the roof or siding, since those act as bridges
  • Fix leaks and improve ventilation in damp areas, especially if you are seeing large black ants
  • Do not disturb fire ant mounds, and keep children and pets away from them

Questions

Ants questions we get asked

Why did the ants come back somewhere else?

Because the colony is still there. Surface sprays kill foragers and can cause some species to split the colony and relocate, which looks like the problem moving rooms.

Do carpenter ants damage a house like termites?

They work more slowly and they do not eat the wood, but they hollow out galleries in it, and the damp wood they choose is often a structural problem in its own right. Both are worth taking seriously.

Can fire ants be eliminated from my yard completely?

Realistically the goal is control rather than permanent elimination, because new colonies move in from surrounding property. A managed yard stays usable; an untreated one does not.
