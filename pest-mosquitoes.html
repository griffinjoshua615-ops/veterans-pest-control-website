Mosquitoes

Mosquito & Tick Control in Selma & Central Alabama

Mosquito control is a yard problem before it is a spraying problem. Alabama's season is long and wet, and a container holding water for about a week is enough to produce a generation. Treatment that ignores the water is treatment you repeat forever.

Signs to look for

How to tell if you have mosquitoes

  • Biting pressure concentrated at dawn and dusk, or all day in heavily shaded yards
  • Mosquitoes rising out of shrubs, ivy or tall grass when disturbed
  • Standing water in gutters, saucers, tarps, buckets, tires, toys or low spots
  • Ticks found on pets after time in the yard, especially around ears and neck
  • Parts of the yard that have simply become unusable in the evening

Why it matters here

What mosquitoes mean for an Alabama property

The breeding sites are usually on the property

Gutters holding water, saucers under plant pots, tarps, buckets, toys, tires, birdbaths, clogged drains and low spots. Many of the mosquitoes biting you in the evening were produced within yards of where you are standing.

They rest where it is cool and shaded

Adults spend the day in dense shrubs, under decks, in tall grass and along fence lines. Those resting sites, not the open lawn, are where treatment actually does something.

Ticks come with the same territory

Tall grass, leaf litter and brushy edges hold ticks, and the same yard work that reduces mosquito harborage reduces tick pressure. Ticks in the southeast carry real disease risk, so this is worth doing properly.

Before we get there

What you can do yourself

None of this replaces treatment, but it reduces the pressure and makes the work hold longer.

  • Walk the yard weekly and tip out anything holding water, which is the single highest-value thing you can do
  • Keep gutters clear and draining, since clogged gutters are a very common hidden source
  • Change birdbath and pet bowl water every few days
  • Cut back dense ground cover and keep grass trimmed along fence lines and shaded edges
  • Treat pets for ticks through vet-recommended products, and check them after time outdoors

Questions

Mosquitoes questions we get asked

How long does a mosquito treatment last?

It is an interval program through the season rather than a single lasting application, because new adults keep emerging and rain resets conditions. We will tell you the realistic interval for your property.

Will it hurt bees and butterflies?

Treatment should be targeted at resting harborage and kept off blooming plants. Tell us if you keep bees, have a pollinator garden or keep fish, and it gets planned around.

Can you get rid of mosquitoes completely?

No honest company will tell you yes. Neighboring property, creeks and drainage keep producing them. The goal is making your yard usable again, and the water-source work is what makes that hold.
