Bed Bugs

Bed Bug Treatment in Selma & Central Alabama

Bed bugs are not a cleanliness problem. They travel in luggage, secondhand furniture and moving boxes, and they turn up in spotless homes, new apartments and good hotels alike. What they are is stubborn, which is why store-bought sprays usually make them worse by scattering them.

Signs to look for

How to tell if you have bed bugs

  • Small dark spots on sheets, mattress seams or box spring fabric, roughly the size of a marker dot
  • Shed skins, pale and hollow, in seams and cracks
  • Rusty or reddish smears on bedding
  • Live insects, flat and reddish-brown, about the size of an apple seed
  • Bites in a line or cluster, though bites alone are not proof, since reactions vary enormously and other insects bite too
  • A sweet, musty odor in a heavy infestation

Why it matters here

What bed bugs mean for an Alabama property

They hide better than they bite

A bed bug spends most of its life in a crack. Mattress seams, box spring frames, headboard joints, outlet plates, loose wallpaper and furniture screw holes all hold them. Treating the mattress alone leaves most of the population untouched.

Retail sprays scatter them

Most consumer aerosols are repellents. Spraying a harborage drives survivors into new rooms and deeper into walls, which turns a one-bedroom problem into a whole-house one and makes the eventual treatment longer.

They survive a long time without feeding

Adults can persist for months without a meal, so leaving a room empty and waiting them out does not work the way people expect.

Before we get there

What you can do yourself

None of this replaces treatment, but it reduces the pressure and makes the work hold longer.

  • Do not throw out the mattress before an inspection, since it rarely solves it and it can spread them through the house on the way out
  • Do not start spraying retail products, because scattering is the main way small problems become large ones
  • Wash and dry bedding on the hottest setting the fabric allows; the dryer does the work, not the wash
  • Keep luggage off beds and floors when traveling, and check the mattress seams of any room you stay in
  • Inspect secondhand furniture carefully before it comes through the door

Questions

Bed Bugs questions we get asked

Do bed bugs mean my house is dirty?

No. They are hitchhikers. Clutter gives them more places to hide, which makes treatment harder, but they arrive on luggage and furniture regardless of how clean a home is.

Can I just treat the bedroom?

Only if the inspection shows that is the extent of it. Bed bugs follow people, so anywhere someone regularly sleeps or sits for long periods needs checking before the scope is set.

How long does it take?

It depends on how far they have spread and how completely the preparation gets done. It is a multi-visit process, and we will give you a realistic schedule after the inspection rather than a number over the phone.
