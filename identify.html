Identify common pests

Not sure what you are seeing? Start here.

Use this quick reference guide for common pests found in Alabama homes and businesses. If you are unsure, send us a photo or request an inspection.

Request an Inspection

Ants

Trails, soil mounds, or wood shavings can indicate activity.

Bed Bugs

Look for dark spots, shed skins, or insects near mattress seams.

Cockroaches

Egg cases, droppings, odor, and nighttime sightings are common signs.

Mosquitoes

Standing water and dense vegetation can support breeding activity.

Rodents

Watch for droppings, gnaw marks, scratching, and shredded nesting material.

Termites

Mud tubes, hollow wood, and indoor swarmers may signal activity.

Spiders

Webs, hiding spots, and recurring activity can require professional control.

Wasps & Hornets

Leave nests alone and request professional removal if they are active.

When in doubt

Do not guess. Let a professional take a look.
